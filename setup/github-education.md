# GitHub Education — apply on day one

GitHub gives verified students a set of paid tools for free. Two of them are
used directly in this course.

**Apply in week 1.** Verification is not instant. It can take a few days, and
longer if your first application is not approved. Apply now and the benefits
are ready when you need them.

Apply here: **<https://github.com/education>**

---

## What you get

| Benefit | Free account | Verified student |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Copilot | Limited | **Free**, including the paid features |
| Codespaces compute | 120 hours a month | **180 hours a month** |
| Codespaces storage | 15 GB-month | **20 GB-month** |
| Private repositories | Limited features | Full GitHub Pro features |

The pack also includes offers from other companies. Those change over time.
The two above are the ones this course uses.

### Why this matters here

**Copilot** is an AI assistant built into VS Code. Week 3 is about working
with an AI assistant, and weeks 4 and 5 depend on one. Copilot is not the
only option, and you are free to use another. See
[`chinese-services.md`](chinese-services.md) for alternatives. But if you
want the one that runs inside your editor, this is how to get it without
paying.

**Codespaces** is the escape hatch in this course. If installing Python on
your own machine fails, you move to a Codespace and lose nothing. See
[`codespaces.md`](codespaces.md). The larger allowance means you are far less
likely to run out of hours during the project weeks.

## Before you apply

You need three things.

**1. A GitHub account.** Create one at <https://github.com>. It is free.

**2. Your university email address, added and verified on that account.**
Go to **Settings** → **Emails**, add your `@leeds.ac.uk` address, then click
the link GitHub sends to it.

Do this first. Many applications are refused only because the academic email
was never added.

**3. Proof that you are a student.** Any one of these:

- a photograph of your student card showing a current enrolment date
- your class timetable
- your transcript
- an enrolment verification letter

Your student card is usually the quickest. Make sure the date is readable in
the photograph.

---

## How to apply

1. Sign in to GitHub.
2. Go to <https://github.com/education>.
3. Click **Start an application**.
4. Give your school name, your academic email address, and your proof of
   enrolment.
5. Submit.

You receive an email when the application is decided.

---

## If your application is not approved

This happens, and it is usually fixable. Common reasons:

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| No academic email on your account | Add and verify your university address, then apply again |
| The date on your document is unreadable | Photograph it again in better light, with the whole document in frame |
| The document does not show current enrolment | Use a document with this academic year on it |
| Your school was not recognised | Reapply and type the full official name of the university |

You may apply again. Wait until you have fixed the reason given in the email.

GitHub's own guidance is at
<https://docs.github.com/en/education/about-github-education/github-education-for-students/apply-to-github-education-as-a-student>.
